# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

Garanty Studios (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting user data. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you download, access, or play our games, software, and services.

---

## 1. Third-Party Platforms & Stores

Our games are distributed through third-party platforms and storefronts (such as Steam and Itch.io). 

- These platforms collect, store, and process their own data (such as payment processing, store analytics, play time, and account management) under their own privacy policies.
- Garanty Studios does not control or take responsibility for data collected independently by these storefronts. For questions regarding platform-level data processing, please consult the respective platform's Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Game Engines, SDKs & Analytics

- **Default Stance:** By default, we do not intentionally build analytical tracking, performance telemetry, or data-gathering SDKs into our software.
- **Engine/SDK Telemetry:** Third-party game engines or integrated software development kits (SDKs) may automatically collect basic performance, system, or crash log data.
- **Product-Specific Policies:** If a specific game or software intentionally includes specialized data collection or third-party ad networks, this will be explicitly stated in that individual product’s documentation or product page.

---

## 3. Data Directly Processed by Garanty Studios

We are solely responsible for the data directly collected and managed by us.

- **Information We Receive:** When you contact us directly via email (`support@garantystudios.net`) or other official contact channels, we may receive personal details such as your name, email address, and message contents.
- **No Selling or Unwanted Sharing:** We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
- **Required Sharing & Transparency:** If sharing data becomes necessary to solve a support ticket or fulfill third-party technical requirements, we will remain transparent and, where applicable, notify you or seek your consent beforehand.

---

## 4. Minors & Children's Privacy

Many of our games and software are targeted at or accessible to minors. 

- Data submitted by or on behalf of minors is handled under the same strict non-sharing and non-commercial principles outlined in this policy.
- Parents or legal guardians who do not comply with or agree to these policies are responsible for preventing their children from accessing our software.
- We strongly advise parents and legal guardians to observe the content warnings, health notices, and platform age ratings provided for each individual game.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights & Requests

We stand fully on the side of our users regarding data control. We will never sell or exploit your personal information for profit.

If you wish to:
- Request access to any personal information we hold about you (e.g., support email logs),
- Request the deletion or correction of your personal data, or
- Ask questions regarding your privacy,

Please contact us, and we will do our best to fulfill your request promptly.
